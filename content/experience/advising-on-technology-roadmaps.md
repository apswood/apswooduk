As a Microsoft Cloud Service Provider (CSP) partner supporting Microsoft 365 and Entra ID, we advised a membership organisation in the transport sector ahead of SMS-based multi-factor authentication retirement.

The standard recommendation in most estates is a mobile authenticator app, but that did not fit how this organisation worked. Members would not install an authenticator app on personal phones and often did not have a separate work phone. Good technical advice has to be grounded in reality, understanding the organisation and its people and working holistically with clients and stakeholders rather than handing over a textbook answer.

Working with the solution architect and account manager, I presented the options to the client's IT lead. We recommended passkeys and hardware security keys as the primary path forward. We also set out that a third-party SMS provider was technically possible, while advising against it on security best practice. Framing the trade-offs around both security and real-world adoption gave the client a clear, workable roadmap.
