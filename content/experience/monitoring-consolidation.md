Following a series of acquisitions, the business was running 44 separate monitoring tools across its managed platforms. Consolidating them onto LogicMonitor formed part of a multi-year integration programme, with cyber security and consistent operational visibility making up a large part of the case for change.

I spent several months working with senior leadership to build the investment case and define the technical requirements. While platform selection, commercial negotiation, technical design and readiness sign-off sat with other leads and teams, I provided technical leadership through the rollout and drove migration completion and the decommissioning of legacy monitoring platforms.

One honest twist stood out once the new platform went live. Accurate thresholds briefly caused priority-one alerts to rise, not because services had degraded, but because the old tools had masked underlying faults. Problem management then worked through those findings systematically to bring alert volumes down and improve underlying stability.
