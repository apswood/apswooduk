I play piano and saxophone, I am learning Spanish and I like travelling in Europe. I am fascinated by how complex systems fit together, from railway routing and signalling to London's hidden history below street level.

I lean towards Scandinavian and Nordic design, and my playlists move between jazz, alternative rock, electronic, pop and Latin music. On screen it is usually Peep Show, The IT Crowd, Detectorists, Taskmaster, The Traitors, Severance or Succession. I also enjoy Nathan Fielder's work and Douglas Adams, and I still have a soft spot for the early web of Homestar Runner and Weebl's Stuff.
